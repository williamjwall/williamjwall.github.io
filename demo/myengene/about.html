FAQ

What is PGx testing?

Examines your DNA to determine how your body metabolizes medications. WGS at 30X depth, 990+ drugs, 1,110 genes.

How does PGx improve treatments?

Replaces trial-and-error with genetically informed prescribing across all specialties.

Can PGx integrate into my workflow?

Order tracking, instant reports, patient management, and healthcare system integrations.

Is patient data secure?

Encrypted storage under HIPAA, GDPR, PIPEDA, and CCPA. Patients control access.

When will I get results?

Typically 5–7 business days, reviewed by clinical pharmacists and licensed providers.
